## Three Qualifying Indiana State Parks

### 1. Chain O'Lakes State Park

**Address:** 2355 E. 75 S., Albion, IN 46701
**Official Website:** https://www.in.gov/dnr/state-parks/parks-lakes/chain-o-lakes-state-park/
**Total Driving Distance:** Approximately **157 miles**

- Dayton to Chain O'Lakes: ~95 miles
- Chain O'Lakes to Notre Dame: ~62 miles[^1]

This park features nine connecting kettle lakes, 29 miles of hiking trails, and numerous recreational activities including boating (electric motors only), fishing, camping, and a historic one-room schoolhouse.[^2][^3][^4]

### 2. Whitewater Memorial State Park

**Address:** 1418 S. State Road 101, Liberty, IN 47353
**Official Website:** https://www.in.gov/dnr/state-parks/parks-lakes/whitewater-memorial-state-park/
**Total Driving Distance:** Approximately **205 miles**

- Dayton to Whitewater Memorial: ~55 miles[^5]
- Whitewater Memorial to Notre Dame: ~150 miles

Established as a memorial to World War II veterans, this park offers access to both 200-acre Whitewater Lake and Brookville Reservoir, along with hiking trails, horseback riding, camping, and various water activities.[^6][^7]

### 3. Pokagon State Park

**Address:** 450 Lane 100 Lake James, Angola, IN 46703
**Official Website:** https://www.in.gov/dnr/state-parks/parks-lakes/pokagon-state-parktrine-state-recreation-area/
**Total Driving Distance:** Approximately **239 miles**

- Dayton to Pokagon: ~160 miles[^8]
- Pokagon to Notre Dame: ~79 miles[^9]

One of Indiana's original state parks (established 1925), Pokagon features natural glacial lakes (Lake James and Snow Lake), hiking trails, the famous winter toboggan run, camping facilities, and the historic Potawatomi Inn.[^10][^11][^12]

All three parks are official Indiana state parks designated and managed by the Indiana Department of Natural Resources, and each offers a total driving distance well under your 260-mile threshold. The direct route from Dayton to Notre Dame is approximately 197 miles for comparison, making each of these detours a reasonable addition to your trip.[^13][^14]
