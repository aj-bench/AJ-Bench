## A. PyMOL: The Standard for Publication-Quality Imagery

### Technical Profile & Access

* **Developer:** Resource for Biocomputing, Visualization, and Informatics (RBVI), University of California, San Francisco (UCSF).<sup>3</sup>

### Technical Profile & Access

* **Developer:** Theoretical and Computational Biophysics Group, University of Illinois at Urbana-Champaign (UIUC).<sup>5</sup>

## D. Mol* (Molstar): The Modern Standard for Web-Based Data Exploration at Scale

* **Pricing Model:** Free and open-source.<sup>7</sup>
