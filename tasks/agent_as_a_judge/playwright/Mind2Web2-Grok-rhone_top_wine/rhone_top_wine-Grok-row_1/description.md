Flavors include black cherry and pepper, with silky texture. Its repeat appearance underscores Beaucastel's reliability, making it approachable for newcomers while satisfying experts.
- **Domaine du Pegau Châteauneuf-du-Pape Cuvée Réservée 2020 (Southern Rhône, 2023 #10)**: Laurence Féraud's traditional cuvée (80% Grenache) emphasizes non-interventionist methods, resulting in rustic, spice-driven profiles.
