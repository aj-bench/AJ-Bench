## Methodology for Data Reconstruction

A complete and transparent methodology is essential for understanding the sponsorship landscape, particularly given the variability in data availability across the analyzed period. Official, tiered sponsorship lists were readily available for the CVPR conferences in 2023, 2024, and 2025\.<sup>15</sup> However, for the years 2019, 2020, 2021, and 2022, the official sponsor-specific webpages were inaccessible, necessitating a meticulous reconstruction of sponsorship data from a wide range of ancillary sources.<sup>18</sup>

To build a comprehensive history for these earlier years, this analysis synthesized information from official press releases, corporate blog posts announcing participation, news articles covering the events, and sponsorship acknowledgements in workshop materials. Where a specific sponsorship tier was explicitly mentioned—such as Microsoft's "Diamond" sponsorship in 2020 or Google's "Platinum" sponsorship in 2019—it has been recorded as such.<sup>21</sup> In instances where a company was confirmed as a sponsor or a major exhibitor without a specified tier, the entry is marked as "Sponsor" or "Exhibitor" to reflect the nature of the available evidence.<sup>13</sup> This rigorous, source-based reconstruction allows for a reliable, albeit partially incomplete, view of the sponsorship ecosystem during the period of disruption caused by the global pandemic. This analysis has identified a core group of 12 companies that have demonstrated a sustained commitment by supporting at least four CVPR conferences between 2019 and 2025\.
