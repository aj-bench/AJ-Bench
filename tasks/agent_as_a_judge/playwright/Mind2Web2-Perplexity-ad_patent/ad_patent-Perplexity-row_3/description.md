### Patent 1: US-12,039,867-B2

**Title:** System and Methods of Adaptive Object-based Decision Making for Autonomous Driving[^1]

**Inventors:** Biao Ma, Lior Tal[^1]

**Organization/Assignee:** Cyngn Inc[^1]

**Filing Date:** August 5, 2022[^1]

**Publication Date:** July 16, 2024[^2][^3]

**Google Patents Link:** https://patents.google.com/patent/US12039867B2/en

This patent covers Cyngn's innovative system that enhances autonomous vehicles' capability to make informed decisions regarding their surroundings. The method enables AVs to not only detect objects but also make informed decisions on interacting with them safely, such as navigating around slow-moving barriers, cyclists, and blind spots near large vehicles. The patent integrates specific driving rules with broader traffic regulations to ensure safe and legally compliant autonomous driving.[^3][^2]

### Patent 2: US-12,032,099-B2

**Title:** Adaptive Motion Compensation of Perception Channels[^4][^5]

**Inventors:** Biao Ma, Lior Tal[^5]

**Organization/Assignee:** Cyngn Inc[^5]

**Filing Date:** July 12, 2022[^5]

**Publication Date:** July 9, 2024[^4][^3]

**Google Patents Link:** https://patents.google.com/patent/US12032099B2/en

This patent introduces a method to maintain the accuracy of data collected by an autonomous vehicle's sensors as the vehicle moves. The system optimizes sensor data processing by integrating advanced LiDAR and image technologies, calculating and adjusting for time delays between data acquisition and processing, and dynamically generating motion models based on real-time sensor movements.[^4][^5]

### Patent 3: US-12,157,479-B2

**Title:** Autonomous Vehicle Safety Platform System and Method[^6]

**Inventors:** Jason Ye, John Cavicchio, Andres Tamez, Jacob Lucero, Justin Tesmer, Anush Gandra, Yaxin Luan, Shane DeMeulenaere[^6]

**Organization/Assignee:** May Mobility Inc[^6]

**Filing Date:** April 28, 2023[^6]

**Publication Date:** December 3, 2024[^6]

**Google Patents Link:** https://patents.google.com/patent/US12157479B2/en

This patent describes a comprehensive safety platform system for autonomous vehicles that includes a low-level safety platform designed to facilitate fallback planning and execution. The system functions to transition autonomous agents between primary (autonomous) operation mode and fallback operation mode, ensuring safe operation even when the vehicle's computing system fails or loses communication abilities.[^6]

All three patents represent significant advances in autonomous driving technology, covering critical areas such as decision-making algorithms, sensor data processing, and safety systems that are essential for the deployment of reliable autonomous vehicles.
