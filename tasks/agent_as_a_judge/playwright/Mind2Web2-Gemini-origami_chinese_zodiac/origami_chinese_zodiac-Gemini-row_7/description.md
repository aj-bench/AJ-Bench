### 1. The Rat (鼠 shǔ): The Quick-Witted Pioneer
- Your Folding Guide: The Origami Rat
  - Source: Instructables 6

  - Instructions:

    2. Unfold the paper. Fold both the left and right corners to meet at the center crease line.
    3. Flip the paper over.
    4. Fold the bottom point up to meet the top point.
    5. Flip the paper over again.
    6. Place a finger inside one of the flaps and press it down flat, creating a squash fold. Repeat on the second flap.
    7. Unfold the point you just made, returning the paper to a diamond shape.
    8. Fold the top point down to the bottom of the small triangle in the middle.
    9. Fold the side corners in to meet at the middle point. Repeat for both sides.
    10. Unfold the top point.
    11. Fold the top point over to meet the left side point.
    12. Unfold the previous step. Gently ease out the paper from the crease and press it down.
    13. Push your finger under the flap at the marked point and crease the paper down.
    14. Open the flap slightly, being careful to maintain its shape. Ease out the extra paper and re-crease so that the new point touches the center line.
    15. Fold the first layer of the right point up to the top point.
    16. Unfold the crease. Open the triangle slightly and tuck the triangle flap into the pocket behind it. Close the top point back down over it.
    17. Flip the paper over. Fold the top triangle down. Flip it over again.
    18. Fold the top point up.
    19. Take the left middle triangle and fold it up, then towards the middle. Unfold. Fold it in half towards the right. Unfold. Fold the sides of the triangle together, manipulating the point to touch the top corner. Repeat for the second triangle.
    20. Fold the entire model in half.
    21. Fold the top flap upward to form a leg. Repeat on the other side.
    22. To create the feet, fold the tail all the way up to the head to make a crease, then unfold. Repeat on the other side.
    23. Open the body slightly and fold inward along the crease made in the previous step. Repeat on the other side.
    24. Open the tail slightly. Fold the tail down, then up. Fold both flaps in the tail towards the center, then fold them to the center again to make the tail thinner.
    25. To form the ears, push your finger into one of the ear flaps and fold it down. Repeat on the other ear. Gently pull the head down, creasing the small triangles at the chest to help shape it.

### 3. The Tiger (虎 hǔ): The Brave and Confident

  - Source: Red Ted Art 10
  - Details: 10 Steps, Photo-Based, Difficulty: Beginner.
  - Instructions:

    9. Use a black pen to draw the Tiger's features: a nose, a mouth, eyes, and whiskers.

### 4. The Rabbit (兔 tù): The Kind and Elegant
- Your Folding Guide: The Origami Rabbit
  - Source: Origami Instructions 11
  - Details: 10 Steps, Photo-Based, Difficulty: Beginner.
  - Instructions:
    1. Start with the colored side of the paper facing down.
    2. Fold the paper in half diagonally to form a triangle.
    3. Unfold the paper back into a square.
    4. Fold both the left and right sides in to meet the center crease.

    8. Fold the entire model in half lengthwise.
    9. Because of the cut, you can now fold the two flaps up on both sides.
    10. These two flaps form the Rabbit's long ears, completing the model.

### 5. The Dragon (龙 lóng): The Confident and Enthusiastic
- Your Folding Guide: The Simple Origami Dragon
  - Source: Instructables 3
  - Details: 18 Steps, Photo-Based, Difficulty: Intermediate-Beginner.
  - Instructions:
    1. Start with a square piece of paper, white side up.
    2. Fold the paper in half diagonally to make a triangle.
    3. Fold this triangle in half again.
    4. Open one of the triangle's flaps and squash it down into a square shape. Repeat on the other side.
    5. With the open end of the square pointing down, fold the two top-layer side edges to the center crease. Then fold the top triangle down. Crease well, then unfold all three folds.
    6. Lift the bottom point of the top layer up, opening the sides and folding them inward along the creases just made, creating a long diamond shape. This is a "bird base." Repeat on the other side.
    7. (Optional but recommended) Open the entire model out. Find the small square of creases in the center. Gently push this square inward, allowing the model to collapse back on its original folds. This "sink fold" creates the dragon's back.
    8. Fold the top flaps down along the existing creases.
    9. Fold the open flaps on one side of the model so they cross over the center line, forming an "X".
    10. Unfold the "X" and collapse the paper inward along these new creases. This new flap will become a wing. Repeat on the other side.
    11. Fold the bottom half of the model outward on both sides to create creases, then unfold.
    12. Perform an "inside reverse fold" on both bottom points, pushing them inward so they point up inside the model. One will become the head/neck, the other the tail.
    13. Lift one of the small flaps on the side of the model up, and collapse it to form a leg. Repeat for all four legs.
    14. Fold the two large wing flaps up at a pleasing angle.
    15. On one of the tall points, perform two successive inside reverse folds to create the neck and head. Fold the very tip back to create a horn.
    16. Fold the four leg flaps down to create the feet.
    17. (Optional) Add a few "crimp folds" (alternating mountain and valley folds) to the tail to give it a curve.
    18. Adjust the wings, head, and tail to give your dragon a unique and dynamic pose.

### 6. The Snake (蛇 shé): The Wise and Mysterious
- Your Folding Guide: The Origami Snake
  - Source: VmathLive 13
  - Details: 13 Steps, Diagram-Based, Difficulty: Beginner.
  - Instructions:
    1. Start with a square piece of paper, white side up. Fold it in half diagonally, then unfold.
    2. Fold the upper right and lower right edges to meet the center crease, forming a kite shape.
    3. Fold the two long edges of the kite in to meet the center crease again, creating a narrow kite.
    4. Fold the upper left and lower left edges in to meet the center crease.
    5. Fold the top half of the model down over the bottom half.
    6. To form the head, fold the left end of the model up and back to create a crease, then unfold. Fold it forward along the same crease, and unfold again.
    7. Gently separate the layers and push the head inward along the creases just made (an inside reverse fold).
    8. Repeat this process on the very tip of the head to create a smaller, more defined head shape.
    9. Along the body of the snake, make four new creases, folding back and forth to make them flexible.
    10. At the first crease behind the head, perform an inside reverse fold to make the body curve down.
    11. At the second crease, perform an inside reverse fold to make the body curve up.
    12. Repeat this process for the remaining creases, creating an "S" shape in the snake's body.
    13. Add eyes with a pen to complete your snake.

### 7. The Horse (马 mǎ): The Energetic and Animated

  - Source: Origami-Fun 14
  - Details: 10 Steps, Diagram-Based, Difficulty: Beginner.
  - Instructions:
    1. Start with a square piece of paper, colored side up. Fold and unfold in half both ways.
    2. Flip the paper to the white side. Fold and unfold in half diagonally both ways.
    3. Using the creases, bring the three top corners down to the bottom corner, collapsing the paper into a square base (preliminary base).
    4. With the open end at the bottom, fold the top layer's outer corners to the center line. Fold the top triangle down, crease, and then unfold all.
    5. Using scissors, cut the top layer only, from the bottom point up to the horizontal crease.
    6. Fold the two "legs" you just cut upward.
    7. Fold these sections in half toward the center.
    8. Turn the model over and repeat steps 4-7 on the other side.
    9. Rotate the model. Make creases for the head and tail as shown in the diagram, then unfold.
    10. Perform an inside reverse fold on both ends to form the head and tail, completing the horse.
