#### Detailed Player Profiles

**Stephen Curry: Revolutionizing the Game and Global Outreach**  
Stephen Curry, born March 14, 1988, in Akron, Ohio, has redefined basketball with his unparalleled three-point shooting, earning him the moniker "Chef Curry." Drafted seventh overall by the Golden State Warriors in 2009, Curry has led the team to four NBA championships (2015, 2017, 2018, 2022) and holds records for most three-pointers made in a season (402 in 2015-16) and career (over 3,700 as of 2025). His All-Star selections span 11 years: 2014, 2015, 2016, 2017, 2018, 2019, 2021, 2022, 2023, 2024, and 2025, reflecting his sustained impact despite injury setbacks in 2020. Any five examples include the 2015 All-Star Game in New York, where he scored 15 points; 2016 in Toronto (26 points); 2017 in New Orleans (27 points); 2018 in Los Angeles (11 points); and 2019 in Charlotte (17 points). Curry's Wikipedia page (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stephen_Curry) details his accolades, including two MVP awards (2015, 2016).

Curry's engagements in China are tied to his Under Armour endorsement, signed in 2013, which has made him a staple in the Asian market. While he participated in preseason games in China in 2017 (Warriors vs. Timberwolves in Shenzhen and Shanghai), his most recent basketball-related tour occurred in 2025. The Curry Brand World Tour culminated in Chongqing from August 18-20, featuring Curry Camp for youth training, Curry Con—an interactive basketball culture event—and sneaker promotions. This marked his only Asian stop in 2025, drawing massive crowds and boosting local sneaker sales. A confirming article from iChongqing (https://www.ichongqing.info/2025/08/14/stephen-currys-china-tour-cranks-up-chinese-sneaker-market/) describes the event's impact on the Chinese market, where Curry reunited with teammate De'Aaron Fox for clinics and fan interactions. Earlier tours, like in 2015 and 2016, involved similar youth programs, but the 2025 event stands out for its scale post-pandemic. Curry has never played professionally in the CBA or held roles there, maintaining focus on NBA and international philanthropy.
