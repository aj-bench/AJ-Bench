#### Detailed Recipe Breakdown: Smashed Cucumber Salad
This dish takes about 25 minutes, including resting time, and serves 4 as a side. It's praised for its simplicity and addictive flavor profile, blending tangy, sesame notes with herbal freshness from cilantro and scallions. Variations across sources include adding chili flakes for heat (if you buy them) or bell peppers for color, but the core remains focused on your ingredients.

**Preparation Steps**:
1. Bash the 500g cucumbers until they split open using a rolling pin or mallet.
2. Cut into 2.5cm/1" chunks and place in a bowl.
3. Sprinkle with 3/4 tsp salt, toss, and let sit for 20 minutes to draw out excess water.
4. Drain the liquid.
5. Mix the dressing: 1.5 tbsp rice vinegar (or your vinegar), 2 tsp sesame oil, 1.5 tsp grated ginger, 2 tsp soy sauce, and 1/2 tsp sugar.
6. Drizzle over the cucumbers, add 2 tbsp chopped cilantro and 1/4 cup sliced scallions, toss, and serve immediately.

#### Detailed Recipe Breakdown: Braised Chicken with Potatoes
This braise takes about 1 hour (plus marinating), serving 4, and results in tender chicken with soft, sauce-soaked potatoes. It's adaptable for stir-fry if you prefer quicker cooking, but braising enhances flavors. Chicken thighs are ideal as they stay juicy; sources note using bone-in for richer broth.

**Preparation Steps**:
1. Marinate 800g chicken thighs with 1 tsp salt, 2 tbsp oyster sauce, 2 tsp light soy sauce, 2 tsp dark soy sauce, 1 tbsp Chinese wine, 1 tbsp sesame oil, 1 tsp sugar, and pepper for 1 hour.
2. Peel and quarter 4 potatoes; slice ginger and cut 2 stalks scallions into sections.
3. Fry potatoes in hot oil until lightly browned, then set aside.
4. In the same wok, fry ginger, scallions, and 6 garlic cloves until fragrant.
5. Add chicken and brown for 3-4 minutes.
6. Add water to cover, bring to boil, then simmer for 30 minutes.
7. Add potatoes midway, simmer until tender, adjust seasoning, and thicken with corn starch if desired.
8. Garnish with sliced scallions.
