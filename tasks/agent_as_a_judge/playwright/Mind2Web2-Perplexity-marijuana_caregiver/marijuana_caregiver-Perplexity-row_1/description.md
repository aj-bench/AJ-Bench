# Medical Marijuana Caregiver Laws Across 10 U.S. States

**Key Takeaway:** Caregiver regulations vary significantly by state—especially regarding patient limits, age requirements, residency, and cultivation rights—necessitating careful review by prospective caregivers and patients alike.

## California

- **Max patients per caregiver:** Up to 5 patients, provided all reside in the same county.[^1]
- **Minimum age:** 18 years (or parent/guardian of a minor).[^1]
- **State residency required:** Yes (must provide California-issued ID).[^1]
- **Cultivation allowed:** Yes.
- **Max plants per patient:** 6 mature or 12 immature plants per patient.[^1]


## Florida

- **Max patients per caregiver:** Generally 1 patient (exceptions for parents of multiple minors).[^2]
- **Minimum age:** 21 years.[^2]
- **State residency required:** Yes.[^2]
- **Cultivation allowed:** No.[^3]
- **Max plants per patient:** N/A (cultivation prohibited).


## New York

- **Max patients per caregiver:** Up to 5 patients.[^4]
- **Minimum age:** 21 years (no minimum if caregiver is parent/guardian of a minor).[^4]
- **State residency required:** Yes.[^4]
- **Cultivation allowed:** Yes, but only one caregiver may cultivate at a time.[^5]
- **Max plants per patient:** 6 mature and 6 immature plants per patient (total 12 plants).[^5]


## Arizona

- **Max patients per caregiver:** Up to 5 patients.[^6]
- **Minimum age:** 21 years.[^6]
- **State residency required:** Yes.[^7]
- **Cultivation allowed:** Yes, if patient lives >25 miles from a dispensary.[^6]
- **Max plants per patient:** 12 plants.[^6]


## Colorado

- **Max patients per caregiver:** Up to 5 qualifying patients.[^8]
- **Minimum age:** 18 years.[^8]
- **State residency required:** Yes.[^8]
- **Cultivation allowed:** Yes.
- **Max plants per patient:** 6 plants (3 mature flowering) per patient; caregivers may cultivate more in aggregate with registry.[^9]


## Texas

- **Max patients per caregiver:** Only minors via parent/guardian; effectively 1 patient.[^10]
- **Minimum age:** No statutory minimum, but caregiver must be parent or legal guardian.[^10]
- **State residency required:** Yes (parent/legal guardian must be Texas resident).[^10]
- **Cultivation allowed:** No.[^10]
- **Max plants per patient:** N/A (cultivation prohibited).


## Pennsylvania

- **Max patients per caregiver:** Up to 5 patients.[^11]
- **Minimum age:** 21 years (or under 21 with PADOH permission).[^11]
- **State residency required:** Yes.[^11]
- **Cultivation allowed:** No.[^12]
- **Max plants per patient:** N/A.


## Ohio

- **Max patients per caregiver:** Up to 2 patients (waivers may allow more).[^13]
- **Minimum age:** 21 years.[^13]
- **State residency required:** Yes.[^13]
- **Cultivation allowed:** No.[^14]
- **Max plants per patient:** N/A.


## Washington

- **Max patients per caregiver:** 1 patient.[^15]
- **Minimum age:** 21 years.[^15]
- **State residency required:** No explicit requirement noted.[^15]
- **Cultivation allowed:** Yes, as designated provider.
- **Max plants per patient:** 6 plants by default; up to 15 if practitioner-authorized.[^16]


## Illinois

- **Max patients per caregiver:** 1 patient.[^17]
- **Minimum age:** 21 years.[^17]
- **State residency required:** Yes.[^17]
- **Cultivation allowed:** No.[^18]
- **Max plants per patient:** N/A.
