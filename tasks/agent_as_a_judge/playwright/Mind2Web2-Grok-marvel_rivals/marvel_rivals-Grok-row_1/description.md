#### Hulk
Hulk's abilities are divided across his forms: the human Bruce Banner for setup and ranged support, Hero Hulk for standard brawling, and Monster Hulk for enhanced destruction after using his ultimate. This multi-form design allows for adaptive playstyles, from preparatory gamma boosts to smashing through enemies. The team-up abilities integrate well with allies like Doctor Strange or Rocket Raccoon for amplified effects. Here's the full list:

- **Gamma Ray Gun**: Normal attack in Bruce Banner form – Fires gamma energy projectiles for ranged damage.
- **Puny Banner**: Ultimate in Bruce Banner form – Transforms into Hero Hulk, boosting health and unlocking new abilities.
- **Gamma Grenade**: Active ability in Bruce Banner form – Launches a grenade that deals area damage and applies gamma radiation effects.
- **Heavy Blow**: Normal attack in Hero Hulk and Monster Hulk forms – Powerful melee punches that build rage.
- **Gamma Burst**: Secondary attack in Hero Hulk and Monster Hulk forms – Releases a burst of gamma energy for close-range blasts.
- **Indestructible Guard**: Active ability in Hero Hulk form – Creates a defensive stance that reduces incoming damage and reflects projectiles.
- **Radioactive Lockdown**: Active ability in Hero Hulk and Monster Hulk forms – Traps enemies in a radioactive field, slowing and damaging them over time.
- **Incredible Leap**: Mobility ability in Hero Hulk and Monster Hulk forms – Performs a massive jump to traverse maps or smash down on foes.
- **Hulk Smash!**: Ultimate in Hero Hulk form – Delivers a ground pound that stuns and damages nearby enemies, potentially transforming into Monster Hulk.
- **World Breaker**: Ultimate in Monster Hulk form – An upgraded smash that causes massive area destruction and knockback.
- **Gamma Boost**: Team-up passive – Enhances allied abilities with gamma energy when teamed with compatible heroes like Doctor Strange.
- **Gamma Fastball**: Team-up ability – Allows Hulk to throw an ally (like Wolverine) as a projectile for surprise attacks.

This extensive kit makes Hulk one of the most ability-rich characters, with transformations adding layers of complexity to his Vanguard role.

- **Lightforce Dagger**: Normal attack in Dagger mode – Throws light-infused daggers for precise damage.
- **Shadow’s Embrace**: Active ability in Dagger mode – Envelops allies in shadows for temporary invulnerability and healing.
- **Veil of Lightforce**: Active ability in Dagger mode – Creates a protective veil that shields and restores health to teammates.
- **Dagger Storm**: Secondary attack in Dagger mode – Unleashes a barrage of daggers in a cone for multi-target damage.
- **Darkforce Cloak**: Normal attack in Cloak mode – Absorbs enemy health with dark tendrils.
- **Light’s Embrace**: Active ability in Cloak mode – Channels light to heal and cleanse debuffs from allies.
- **Terror Cape**: Active ability in Cloak mode – Deploys a cape that fears and slows enemies in an area.
- **Dark Teleportation**: Secondary attack in Cloak mode – Teleports short distances, leaving a damaging shadow trail.
- **Eternal Bond**: Shared ultimate – Merges light and dark forces to create a massive area effect that heals allies and damages enemies.
- **From Shadow to Light**: Team-up passive – Boosts team mobility and grants brief invisibility when paired with heroes like Black Widow.

Their 10 abilities provide versatile support, making them excellent for team compositions focused on sustain and positioning.

#### Iron Fist
As a Duelist, Iron Fist's abilities draw from martial arts traditions, featuring combo attacks, recovery mechanics, and high mobility. His passive allows chi buildup for empowered strikes, while the team-up adds elemental flair. This kit supports aggressive, fluid combat with opportunities for wall-running and leaps. Full list:

- **Jeet Kune Do**: Normal attack – Rapid punches that chain into combos for building damage.
- **Yat Jee Chung Kuen**: Secondary attack – A charged fist strike that pierces defenses and knocks back enemies.
- **Living Chi**: Ultimate – Channels inner energy for a massive area blast, healing self and boosting speed.
- **K'un-Lun Kick**: Active ability – Delivers a flying kick that stuns and launches opponents.
- **Harmony Recovery**: Active ability – Meditates to restore health and chi over time.
- **Crane Leap**: Mobility ability – Performs an aerial leap for evasion or closing gaps.

- **Wall Runner**: Mobility ability – Runs along walls for enhanced navigation and surprise flanks.
- **Chi Absorption**: Passive – Absorbs energy from hits to empower future abilities.
- **Dragon's Chill**: Team-up passive – Infuses attacks with ice when teamed with Luna Snow, slowing enemies.

With 10 abilities, Iron Fist offers a balanced mix of offense, defense, and mobility, rewarding skilled players who master his combos.
