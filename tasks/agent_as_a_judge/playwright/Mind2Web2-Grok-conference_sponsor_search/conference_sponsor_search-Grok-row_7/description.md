#### Sponsorship Trends and Analysis

Sponsorship tiers typically include Diamond/Champion (highest), Platinum/Spotlight, Gold/Premium, and Silver/Standard (lowest), with benefits like booth space, branding, and speaking opportunities scaling accordingly. From 2019 to 2025, tiers evolved slightly, with "Platinum" becoming the top tier in recent years, replacing "Diamond" in earlier editions. Major sponsors like Amazon and Microsoft often maintain high tiers, indicating sustained commitment to the field. Smaller or specialized companies like Kitware appear in lower tiers but consistently support the event.

The COVID-19 pandemic affected 2020 and 2021 conferences, which were virtual, potentially influencing sponsorship levels, but big players continued participation. Post-2022, as events returned to hybrid/in-person formats, sponsorship increased, with 2024 and 2025 seeing record exhibitors. Chinese companies like Baidu and Alibaba were prominent in 2019 but less so during U.S.-China trade tensions, reappearing in 2023-2025. Autonomous vehicle firms like Tesla and Toyota emphasize practical applications, sponsoring to advance robotics and driving tech.
