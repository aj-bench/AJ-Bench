# Five Scholarships and Fellowships for Female Graduate Students in Computer Science

## 1. AAUW American Dissertation Fellowship

The American Association of University Women (AAUW) American Dissertation Fellowship supports women doctoral candidates completing their dissertations. This fellowship specifically encourages applicants engaged in science, technology, engineering, and math fields, making it ideal for computer science graduate students.[^1][^2][^3]

**Award Amount:** \$25,000[^2]
**Application Deadline:** November 30 (extended from November 15)[^1][^2]

## 2. Society of Women Engineers (SWE) Graduate Scholarship

The Society of Women Engineers offers scholarships to women pursuing graduate degrees in engineering, engineering technology, and computer science. The program supports both master's and PhD candidates enrolled in ABET-accredited programs.[^4][^5][^6]

**Award Amount:** Varies, typically ranging from \$1,000 to \$15,000[^7][^4]
**Application Deadline:** Varies by specific scholarship, but typically February through May[^8][^5]

## 3. Microsoft Research Women's Fellowship

The Microsoft Research Women's Fellowship Program provides funding to select universities to award fellowships to outstanding women pursuing PhDs in computer science and related fields. This program specifically targets women in their second year of graduate studies and must be applied for during the first year.[^9][^10][^11]

**Award Amount:** \$20,000 (with \$18,000 toward tuition and \$2,000 for conference travel)[^11]
**Application Deadline:** Varies by participating university (contact department chair)[^11]

## 4. P.E.O. Scholar Award

The P.E.O. Scholar Award is a merit-based fellowship for women pursuing doctoral-level degrees at accredited U.S. or Canadian institutions. While not exclusively for computer science, it supports women in all fields including STEM disciplines.[^12][^13][^14]

**Award Amount:** Up to \$25,000[^14][^12]
**Application Deadline:** November 20 (nomination period: August 20 - November 20)[^13][^14]

## 5. IBM PhD Fellowship

The IBM PhD Fellowship Program supports exceptional PhD students in technology fields, with a particular emphasis on supporting women and underrepresented minorities. The program covers computer science and related technical fields including artificial intelligence, cybersecurity, and data science.[^15][^16][^17]

**Award Amount:** \$40,000 for U.S. universities (previously \$60,000 first year, \$35,000 second year)[^16][^17]
**Application Deadline:** August 30 (nomination window typically July 15 - August 30)[^17][^18]

These fellowships and scholarships represent substantial opportunities for female U.S. citizens enrolled in computer science graduate programs. Each program has specific eligibility requirements and application procedures, so prospective applicants should review the complete guidelines on the respective program websites before applying. The AAUW and P.E.O. programs require nominations, while others allow direct applications through universities or professional organizations.
